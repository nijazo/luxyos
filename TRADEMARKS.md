# Trademark policy

"LuxyOS" and the Ring of light logo are trademarks of Nijaz Omanovic and identify the official LuxyOS project ([LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md)). The code is free under its licences ([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)); the name and logo are protected so that people can trust that something called LuxyOS really is LuxyOS and follows its promises: privacy, no telemetry, and EU law as the baseline.

## LuxyOS is always free

Nobody may charge money for LuxyOS itself, or for downloading it, under the LuxyOS name. Charging for your own work is fine (installation help, support, training, hardware), as long as customers are told that LuxyOS itself is free. See [docs/terms.md](docs/terms.md).

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to LuxyOS in articles, reviews, videos, talks and teaching material.
- Saying that your app or device "works with LuxyOS" or "runs on LuxyOS", if that is true.
- Using the unmodified logo files from [brand/](brand/) to link to the project, in community events or user groups, as long as it is clear you are not the official project.
- Distributing unmodified official LuxyOS releases under the LuxyOS name.

## Needs written permission

- Distributing a **modified** version under the LuxyOS name. Modified versions are welcome, but under a different name and logo, unless they are approved.
- Selling devices with LuxyOS preinstalled, or offering LuxyOS as a managed service, under the LuxyOS name. LuxyOS itself may never be charged for separately.
- Using the name or logo in a product name, company name or domain name.
- Changing the logo or making merchandise.

## Conditions for partners

Partners who may use the LuxyOS name (distributors, device makers, store developers and managed service providers) must:

- keep the privacy promises: no telemetry, no advertising ID, nothing sent without the user's permission;
- follow EU law for their LuxyOS work, wherever they are based ([docs/europe.md](docs/europe.md));
- not add software that weakens security or privacy.
