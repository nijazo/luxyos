**Draft.** A lawyer checks these terms before the first release. LuxyOS is a project of Nijaz Omanovic; no rights can be derived from these terms before release ([LEGAL.md](../LEGAL.md)).

## 1. LuxyOS is free and non-commercial

- LuxyOS is a free operating system for every person. There is no price, no paid edition, no subscription, no advertising and no selling of data.
- Everyone may use it free of charge: private users, schools, associations, governments and businesses.
- The LuxyOS project does not aim to make a profit. Its costs (servers, development, legal advice) are paid from donations, grants for open source (EU and national) and sponsors. Sponsors get no influence on the product and no user data.
- Donations are welcome via PayPal: [paypal.me/nijazo](https://paypal.me/nijazo). A donation is voluntary and gives no rights or influence.
- Security updates are free for everyone, always. Registration stays optional.
- Nobody may charge money for LuxyOS itself, or for downloading it, under the LuxyOS name ([trademark policy](../TRADEMARKS.md)). Charging for your own work is allowed, such as installation help, support, training or hardware, as long as customers are told that LuxyOS itself is free.

The GPL-3.0 licence allows anyone to use the code, including companies. The non-commercial character of LuxyOS is protected by these terms and the trademark policy, not by the licence ([LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md)).

### Supporters list (optional)

- Donors are anonymous by default. A donor appears on the supporters list on luxyos.com only if they ask for it, for example in the PayPal note: "Show: name" (name only) or "Show: name + amount".
- We show only what they chose: the name as they wrote it (a first name or nickname is fine) and, if asked, the amount. Never e-mail addresses or payment details.
- The legal basis is consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). Anyone can withdraw at any time by e-mail; they are removed in the next update of the site.
- The list is not stored in the public GitHub repository, because names in a public Git history cannot be fully removed. It is kept privately by the owner and used only to build the live website.

## 2. Store terms for developers

### Free to list

- Every developer can build software with the free SDK and request a place in the store.
- Listing is free: no registration fee, no yearly fee and **no commission**.

### How a request works

1. Create a free developer account. For paid apps, also give the trader details the Digital Services Act requires: name, address, email, phone and, where applicable, the trade register number. The store shows these details to users.
2. Submit the app with its manifest: permissions, network use and the data the app collects.
3. Automatic checks: malware scan, manifest, sandbox and signature. Then a review against these terms.
4. A decision within a stated time, with reasons. A rejected app can be fixed and submitted again, or the developer can appeal.
5. The store signs and publishes the app. Updates go through the same checks.

### Free and paid apps

- Free and paid apps are both welcome, open source or closed source.
- **The store sells nothing and handles no payments.** For a paid app, the store page shows the price and a "Buy on the developer's website" link.
- The developer sells the app on their own website and handles payment, invoices, VAT, refunds and the 14-day right of withdrawal under EU consumer law.
- After buying, the user activates the app with a licence key or an account, as the developer chooses.
- LuxyOS never takes a percentage of the price.

### Rules for every app

- **Honest:** the description, screenshots and compatibility label match what the app does.
- **Privacy:** follow the GDPR. No tracking or data collection without the user's explicit consent. All data the app collects is declared in the manifest and shown before installation. No selling of user data.
- **Advertising** in an app is declared on the store page and uses no tracking without consent.
- **Safe:** no malware, hidden mining, unauthorised access or attempts to escape the sandbox.
- **AI:** AI features are labelled and follow the administrator's AI setting (blocked, local only or allowed).
- **Legal:** content and app follow EU law ([europe.md](europe.md)).
- The developer keeps all rights to the app. By listing it, the developer allows LuxyOS to host, sign, show and distribute it in the store.

### Removal

- Users can report an app. Apps that break these terms can be removed. The developer gets the reasons and can appeal.
- An app with an urgent security problem is blocked at once, and the developer is told why.

## 3. Open-source apps from GitHub

- Open-source apps are imported automatically when their licence allows redistribution. They carry the label *From GitHub* and go through the same checks.
- The original developers can take over the store page or ask for removal at any time.
