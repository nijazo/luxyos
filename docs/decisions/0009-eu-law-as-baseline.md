# 0009 EU law as the baseline, in every country

**Status:** Proposed

## Context
LuxyOS is made in and for the European Union. People and businesses outside the EU may also use it. A separate, weaker version for other countries would undermine the privacy promise and double the work.

## Decision
LuxyOS follows EU law (GDPR, ePrivacy, Cyber Resilience Act, European Accessibility Act, AI Act, Digital Services Act and others) as the minimum standard, with one version for all countries. Online services run in the EU. Partners who distribute LuxyOS, sell devices with it, offer store apps or provide managed services must follow EU rules for that work, wherever they are based. Details are in [europe.md](../europe.md).

## Consequences
- Users everywhere get the European level of privacy, security and accessibility.
- Requirements from the CRA and the EAA shape the release process and the design from the start: SBOM, vulnerability handling, security updates for the support period, accessibility in every screen.
- The rules for partners are set through store terms, partner agreements and the trademark policy, because an open-source licence cannot impose them on users.
- Before the first release, a lawyer specialised in EU digital law checks the plan.
