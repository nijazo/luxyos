# 0011 Free and non-commercial; a free store without commission

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
LuxyOS is meant to be a free operating system for every person, not a commercial product. Developers should be able to offer their software without paying the platform. Earlier plans mentioned business subscriptions, store commission and income from search engine partners; these do not fit.

## Decision
- LuxyOS is free for everyone, including businesses, with no paid editions, subscriptions, advertising or data sales.
- The project is non-profit, funded by donations, open-source grants and sponsors without influence on the product. The legal form is open (for example a foundation).
- Listing apps in the store is free, with no fees and no commission. The store handles no payments: developers sell paid apps on their own website, and the store page links there.
- Search engines in the choice screen are offered neutrally, without income deals.
- Nobody may charge for LuxyOS itself under the LuxyOS name ([TRADEMARKS.md](../../TRADEMARKS.md)). Charging for own services (installation, support, hardware) is allowed.

Details: [terms.md](../terms.md).

## Consequences
- No payment system, merchant of record or VAT handling in the store; developers handle sales, VAT and consumer rights themselves.
- The GPL-3.0 licence still allows commercial use of the code; the non-commercial character is protected by the terms and the trademark policy.
- Funding must be found before launch. Free business features (Active Directory, central management) need volunteers, grants or sponsors; paid help comes from independent IT partners.
