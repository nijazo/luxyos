# Licence

**Not decided yet.** The licence will be chosen before the first source code is published.

Points for the decision:

- LuxyOS builds on Linux (GPL-2.0) and Wine (LGPL-2.1). Our own components must be compatible with these.
- Candidates for our own code: GPL-3.0-or-later, or a permissive licence such as Apache-2.0 or MIT.
- Candidate for documentation and designs: CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0.
- The LuxyOS name and logo are trademarks and are not covered by the code licence.
- LuxyOS follows EU law in every country ([docs/europe.md](docs/europe.md)). An open-source licence cannot require users to follow EU law; rules for partners (distributors, device makers, store developers) go into the store terms, partner agreements and the trademark policy.

Until a licence is chosen, all rights are reserved by the project owner.
